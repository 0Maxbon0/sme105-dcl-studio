    K-Line / Pin 7
    
      
    
      
    Hardware Required / Not Connected · Hardware RX disabled · TX prohibited
    
      
    Pin 7 ISO9141 is separate from DCL and STO. A dedicated automotive K-Line transceiver is required. MAX487 and BSS138 are unsuitable. GPIO19 is an unconnected future candidate, with no qualified receiver backend.
    
      
    No modules or services have been detected. ABS is not fitted. Central locking is fitted, but independent K-Line access is unverified. PATS is future read-only information only.
    
      
    Passive silence is expected without an external diagnostic session; silence alone is not an ECU failure. No initialization, wake-up, scanning, retries or transmit operations are implemented.
    
      
      
    ASCII: KLINE t_us=100 byte=55. JSONL requires channel KLINE and uint32 t_us, with byte, edge, error or metadata. Binary imports use synthetic byte-index timestamps. All imports remain unverified offline evidence.
